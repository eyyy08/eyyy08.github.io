What is Crochet?

Crochet is a craft that uses a single hook to create loops of yarn that are pulled through one another to form fabric. Each stitch is worked one at a time, which makes crochet very forgiving and easy to adjust as you go.

Because you are only working with one active stitch at a time, crochet is easier to pause, pick back up, and fix than many other fibre crafts. If something does not look right, you can simply pull back a few stitches and try again.

What You'll Need?

Hook

Used to create stitches and loops.

Yarn

The main material for your project.

Stitch Marker

Helps you keep track of your stitches.

Scissors

Used to cut yarn when needed.

Yarn Needle

Used to weave in loose ends.

Measuring Tape

Helps you check the size and length.

Type of Hook

  • Inline
  • Tapered

Acrylic

Affordable, lightweight, easy to care for.

Cotton

Soft, breathable, highly absorbent.

Wool

Elastic, warm and cozy.

Inline Hook

Head of the hook is in-line with the handle, deeper mouth groove, slightly pointed tip.

Tapered Hook

Head of the hook extends beyond the handle, rounder/smoother mouth groove that isn't as deep, rounder tip.
